# Code of Conduct

Wallstreet is a learning-oriented open-source project. Everyone should feel comfortable asking beginner questions and discussing technical decisions.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive, especially when someone is new to Python or finance.
- Explain disagreements with examples and evidence. Critique ideas or code, not people.
- Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish someone else's personal information, financial details, or private messages.
- Do not harass, insult, threaten, or discriminate against contributors.
- Avoid unsolicited financial promotions, referral spam, and claims of guaranteed returns.

## Where this applies

These expectations apply to repository issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you see harmful conduct, ask the maintainer for a private reporting channel through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Thavarshan). **Do not post sensitive details or personal information in a public issue.** If the issue is a technical vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, request changes, limit participation, or block accounts to keep the project safe. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical, without promising absolute confidentiality.
